# Mining-site RAG test questions (2026-10-15). One question per line.
When did mining begin at the site, and what was mined?
Which companies are identified as responsible, and how were they connected to the mining?
What harm to human health is documented, and who was affected?
What concerns did the public raise about the cleanup plan, and how did EPA respond?
How did the cleanup plan change over time, and why?
